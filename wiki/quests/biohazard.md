# Biohazard

| Detail     | Info     |
|------------|----------|
| Difficulty | Novice   |
| Length     | Long     |
| Quest Points | 3 QP  |

## Requirements

- Completion of Plague City

## Items Needed

- Items obtained during the quest (pigeon cage, rotten apple, doctor's gown)

## Rewards

- 3 Quest Points
- 1,250 Thieving XP
- Access to King Lathas' training field (Combat Training Camp)

## Walkthrough

1. Speak to **Elena** in **East Ardougne**. She needs you to help retrieve her distillator from the Mourners and smuggle plague samples to Varrock for analysis.
2. In **Jerico's** house (north of the East Ardougne chapel), search the **cupboard** for **birdfeed** (after talking to Jerico), and pick up the **pigeon cage** behind the house.
3. Use the **birdfeed** on the **watchtower** by the Mourner headquarters (2563, 3304): "You throw a hand full of seeds onto the watch tower".
4. Then, standing right by the watchtower (x 2559-2565, z 3299-3307), **Open** the pigeon cage: "The pigeons fly towards the watch tower". Opened anywhere else, or before the birdfeed, "The pigeons don't want to leave" - the cage itself does nothing on the tower.
5. Talk to **Omart**: once the pigeons are out, his **rope ladder** takes you over the wall into West Ardougne. Then use a **rotten apple** on the **cauldron** in the Mourners' yard, poisoning the Mourners' food (only after crossing by the ladder).
6. Search the **cupboard** in **Nurse Sarah's** house for a **doctor's gown** (there once the stew is poisoned).
7. Wearing the doctor's gown, enter the **Mourner headquarters**. Fight **2 Mourners** inside and retrieve Elena's **distillator**.
8. Return the distillator to **Elena**. She prepares sample **vials** for you to deliver.
9. Travel to the **Chemist** west of **Rimmington** and obtain **touch paper** for the analysis.
10. Give the vials to **three children** in East Ardougne. They will smuggle the samples past the Varrock guards for you since the guards will search adult travellers.
11. Travel to Varrock and collect the vials from the children at the **Dancing Donkey Inn**.
12. Wearing priestly attire, visit **Guidor** in Varrock to have the samples analysed.
13. Guidor reveals that the **plague is fabricated** -- the samples show no trace of disease.
14. Report this discovery to **Elena** in Ardougne.
15. Meet **King Lathas** in Ardougne Castle. He reveals a different threat and grants you access to his training field.
